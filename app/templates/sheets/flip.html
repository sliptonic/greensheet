A greensheet has two faces. On the front, {{ sheet.requester.display }} sets out what they need from you.

On the flip side, you set out what you need from {{ sheet.requester.display }}. It works the same way: you add items, they mark them done, and either of you can reach the other about any item.

Nothing on the front changes. {{ sheet.requester.display }} will see the flip side the next time they open the greensheet, and in their daily summary if they've asked for one. Once created, the two faces stay paired.
